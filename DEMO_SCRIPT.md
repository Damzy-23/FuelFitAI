# 🎤 FuelFit AI - Demo Script (30 seconds)

## Opening (5 seconds)
"I built FuelFit AI - it answers: Does this meal fuel your workout, or cost you one?"

## Demo Flow (20 seconds)

**Step 1:** "I ate a burger and did a 30-minute run."

**Step 2:** [Select "Burger" → "Running" → "Fat Loss" → Click Analyze]

**Step 3:** "FuelFit instantly shows me:
- This burger costs me 42 minutes of running to burn
- My FuelScore is 6.2/10 - not great for fat loss
- The AI explains it's high in fat and low in protein
- It suggests swapping fries for sweet potato to save 12 minutes"

## Closing (5 seconds)
"Instead of abstract calories, FuelFit translates food into effort, performance, and recovery - making nutrition instantly understandable and actionable."

---

## Key Points to Highlight

✅ **Workout Cost** - "42 minutes of running" (not "550 calories")
✅ **FuelScore** - Simple 0-10 rating judges love
✅ **AI Coach** - Personalized, actionable feedback
✅ **Smart Suggestions** - Specific swaps, not generic advice
